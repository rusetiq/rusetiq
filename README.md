## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rusetiq/rusetiq/main/4.gif" width="40" /> stuff ive made

### 01 / [vithi](https://vithistudy.web.app/)

**a better way to figure out what you're supposed to be studying**

made this cuz apparently keeping track of school normally wasnt complicated enough

vithi brings your tasks, schedules, study plans and progress into one place, with ai-powered tools and roadmaps that help turn what you want to learn into something you can actually follow

`React` `Cloudflare Workers` `D1` `R2` `Firebase Auth` `Google Calendar` `AI`

[try vithi](https://vithistudy.web.app/)

---

### 02 / [rippl](https://rippl.aarush-uae.workers.dev/app)

**make every action count**

built this to make everyday sustainable choices a little easier to stick with

log an action, upload photo evidence and let gemini check it. tracks estimated carbon and water savings, streaks, badges and points, with a community feed and rewards

`React` `TypeScript` `Cloudflare Workers` `D1` `R2` `Firebase Auth` `Gemini`

[try rippl](https://rippl.aarush-uae.workers.dev/app) · [source code](https://github.com/rusetiq/rippl)

---

### 03 / [eVisa Bharat](https://github.com/rusetiq/bwmi-evisa-bharat)

**india's eVisa experience, but actually usable**

i looked at india's eVisa website and decided yeah i wanna redo this

redesigned the experience to make applying less confusing, faster and usable on basically anything, with application tracking, document handling and an admin workflow

built for **Build What Moves India**, where it made the top 250

`Cloudflare Workers` `full-stack web` `responsive UI` `application workflows`

[source code](https://github.com/rusetiq/bwmi-evisa-bharat)

---

### 04 / [identiq](https://github.com/rusetiq/identiq/)

**attendance, without the whole roll call thing**

a biometric attendance system that uses computer vision to recognize enrolled students, track attendance sessions and manage records

includes enrollment, recognition, session management and a parent portal

built as my class 12 computer science project because apparently a normal database project wasnt enough

`Python` `Flask` `OpenCV` `SQLite` `face recognition`
